Runbook: payments-api Degraded

Covers the Degraded state only. If the service is Offline, page Team Harbour and stop reading.

Symptoms

Captures succeed on the second or third attempt. The status board shows payments-api as Degraded, thirty-day uptime slips below 99.8%, and queue depth on the capture worker climbs steadily rather than sawtoothing.

First checks, in order

  1. Reload the status board: confirm the Degraded state is real and not a stale probe.
  2. Check the deploy log for anything shipped to payments-api in the last hour.
  3. Read queue depth on hbr-app-01 and hbr-app-02.
  4. If a host is Draining, take it out of the rotation properly rather than waiting.

Rollback

A Degraded payments-api caused by a deploy rolls back cleanly. Ship the previous version, wait one full probe interval, and watch the pill go from Degraded back to Operational. Never roll back twice without reading the deploy log first.

Escalation

Thirty minutes Degraded, or any customer-visible failure, goes to Team Harbour — and to Team Meridian at the same time if the ledger is involved, because a retry that lands twice is a reconciliation problem before it is a payments one.
